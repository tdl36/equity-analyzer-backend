# Meeting question source benchmark

Use the supplied ABT July–September 2026 originals for a repeatable human review.
Keep the source set, meeting format, audience, user instructions and framework
revision identical between runs. Record the job and question-set versions.
Do not treat this as a controlled framework experiment if any of those differ.

## Acceptance cases

| Source observation | Required behavior |
| --- | --- |
| JPMorgan September 1, p. 2: Exact salesforces remain separate; international team expansion described | Ask execution, investment and return milestones, rather than asking which salesforce strategy management will choose. Attribute reported management comments appropriately. |
| JPMorgan August 20, p. 1: settlement and substantial remaining claims; analyst discussion of monetization | Do not assert remaining NEC liability is materially resolved. Ask how the settlement changes uncertainty and strategic options. |
| Oppenheimer September 8, p. 1: preliminary checks at one German center | Preserve sample limitation; do not present procedure-time comparison as representative controlled evidence. |
| Earnings call: five upcoming clinical programs | Do not label all five pivotal without additional original support. |
| Earnings call: fifth CGM facility in planning, location unresolved | Distinguish planning from approved investment; probe timing and demand assumptions. |
| Piper August 25, p. 1: Libre Duo FDA development | Use the more recent development; do not confuse device classification with payment codes. |
| Earnings release: GAAP and adjusted earnings differ | Ask persistence and cash reconciliation without assuming the gap narrows. |
| Earnings call: four H2 acceleration drivers | Cover Core Lab as well as Nutrition, EP and Cancer Diagnostics when meeting scope allows; distinguish comparison effects from underlying growth. |

## Meeting utility

- Retain product complementarity and conglomerate economics where relevant.
- Group topics; put priority questions first and optional probes in follow-ups.
- Separate sourced observations from investment interpretations in private context.
- For generalists, explain significance before technical terminology.
- Avoid question-count padding and disclose missing source coverage privately.

Score each case pass, partial or fail with the question and original passage.
Any materially false premise requires revision before meeting use. Citation
matching and unit tests are mechanical checks, not semantic accuracy guarantees.
The September 11 policy update has routing/unit-test coverage; a newly generated
real-source pack still requires this review before claiming quality improvement.
