Charlie · Ticker-to-case workflow

T120 · October 1, 2026. Model-backed research is paid; opening saved content and deterministic calculation do not use model credits. This guide describes delivered behavior and its limits.

1. Enter a ticker and open its case

From Research desk, choose Investment cases & portfolio. In Company, type the ticker (for example AAPL), then click Open investment case. Check the displayed ticker. This is a ticker workspace, not automatic issuer resolution: confirm the legal issuer, listing and currency in your originals.

Expected: An existing company shows its saved revision; a new company shows New investment case. Opening a ticker, Snapshot or a saved report does not start paid research.

If blocked / limits: If the company is wrong, correct it before selecting documents. Unsaved edits block switching companies. Save them, or use Allow reload without saving and reopen the company to actually discard them.

2. Make eligible originals available

Open Deep Research and inspect the stored-original list. Use your existing Charlie document upload or managed collection workflow if the list is empty. For AlphaSense collection, the Mac and local agent must be available, Chrome must be controllable, and AlphaSense must be signed in; complete MFA directly there. Wait for verified handoff/import, then reopen Deep Research.

Expected: The intended originals appear under the correct ticker. Open the originals and check issuer, date, publisher, completeness and AI-use rights. Select 1–8 relevant documents with combined readable text at most 160,000 characters.

If blocked / limits: A file in iCloud alone does not prove it is stored for this workflow. A queued collection is not a download. Restricted originals stay excluded. Unreadable or oversized packs require smaller readable originals; do not treat failure as zero results.

3. Run research for an actual investment question

If you have an existing investment case, save pending edits first. Select the originals, confirm issuer and permitted use, then click Start Deep Research. Use this paid step only for research you actually want, not for a smoke test. Configure your research model key and budget in Settings beforehand.

Expected: A saved research ID appears with the exact case baseline, followed by section and stage progress. The run covers 22 sections in up to six research calls and six review calls. It freezes original and extracted-text hashes. It does not search the web or manufacture missing consensus.

If blocked / limits: An unfinished run blocks a second new run for the ticker. After a timeout inspect saved runs before resubmitting. Resume saved work preserves completed stages; an unknown provider-call outcome requires checking usage and acknowledging a potentially repeated charge. Stop research prevents later stages, not a charge for the current call.

4. Read findings, gaps and sources

Select Saved research revision. Review Snapshot, Full research and Sources & baseline. Expand supporting excerpts, especially material claims. Read management guidance, broker estimates, reported facts and interpretation as different classes. Compare the frozen baseline to your current case.

Expected: Passage matched + model reviewed means the excerpt matched the extracted original and passed a model support review; it is not investor approval or an independent audit. Full research preserves unresolved claims and gaps. Download research keeps them; Download investment map excludes unresolved claims.

If blocked / limits: A complete run can still have significant missing coverage. Do not accept an unsupported claim because it is repeated or appears in a polished export. A changed baseline is visibly flagged; historical research is not silently rewritten.

5. Create or update your investment case

For initiation (research baseline R0, no saved case), use Prepare initial thesis draft · review before saving. Alternatively, in Current thesis use Load saved thesis as a draft or enter your own thesis. Review Investment thesis, Where my view differs, Market expectations and What would change my mind. Add at least one explicit assumption with support, contrary evidence and a next test. Click Save investment case revision when you agree.

Expected: The saved revision increases and Snapshot reflects the saved wording. Research output alone never becomes an accepted case. Original thesis documents remain separate. Save retries of unchanged edits reuse their request identity.

If blocked / limits: For an existing case, use Update thesis · select evidence, then Assess documents in Evidence & proposals (a separate paid comparison). Inspect each proposal before accepting it into an assumption or recording a no-change/rejection decision. Stale baselines block acceptance; reconcile the latest case first.

6. Link base revenue to a research passage

In Operating scenarios choose Add EV/EBITDA model if appropriate. Under Base revenue · source observation click Find revenue in completed research. Choose the run, reported passage and exact printed revenue figure. Enter its printed units, fiscal year, currency, accounting definition and original page/section. Inspect the original, confirm these meanings, then click Check and use revenue in draft.

Expected: The draft receives revenue converted to millions, base fiscal year and currency, plus a frozen passage/hash receipt. A 1.0 billion figure becomes 1,000 million. Only supported reported-fact passages qualify; estimates, guidance and unresolved claims are excluded. The link operation does not save or start research.

If blocked / limits: The number selector can include years and other metrics: you must choose consolidated annual revenue. Period, accounting meaning and issuer are analyst-confirmed, not machine-verified. Missing evidence may remain a clearly manual reference. Changed originals or restrictions block relinking/calculation/save; restore eligible evidence or explicitly unlink. Historical receipts remain historical.

7. Calculate scenarios and save only reviewed inputs

Confirm positive EBITDA is an appropriate valuation basis. Fill the shared base and target fiscal years (1–5 years apart), currency, revenue, reference share price/date and definitions. For bear/base/bull enter revenue CAGR, EBITDA margin, EV/EBITDA, target net debt, other senior claims, nonoperating assets, diluted shares and source-backed rationale. All money and shares are in millions except per-share prices. Click Calculate draft, inspect the results, then Save case with these model inputs when satisfied.

Expected: Revenue → EBITDA → enterprise value → equity → per-share value uses deterministic code, not AI arithmetic. Negative equity is floored at zero with a warning. Reverse valuation states which inputs are held fixed. Editing inputs hides stale results; saving recalculates and attaches inputs/results to one immutable case revision.

If blocked / limits: This template is unsuitable for banks, insurers, REITs and loss-making/recovery situations. Future assumptions remain yours. Price returns exclude dividends, discounting, FX and probabilities. Changing linked revenue/year/currency requires relinking or Unlink revenue evidence · keep the number. Manual mode is not source verification.

8. Check versions, export and recover

After saving, open Snapshot. Confirm the displayed revision and model results. Use Saved version and Compare with an earlier revision to inspect changes. Download investment map exports the selected saved case; Download saved case with evidence · JSON preserves the current saved case. Revision history lets you inspect an earlier case and Restore revision as a new revision.

Expected: Old snapshots and exports retain their own inputs, outputs and evidence receipts. Restoration adds a new revision and preserves intervening history. Research-map exports describe a research run; Snapshot-map exports describe a saved case. Their identifiers are intentionally different.

If blocked / limits: Unsaved edits are not included in a saved Snapshot. Do not restore merely to test on valuable research; use it when you genuinely want to restore that version. A newer case saved on another device causes a conflict, preserving your local edits for reconciliation.

9. Track the next test and return to the same case

Use Case signals to record dated observations and falsification tests, Decisions & underweights for your decision rationale and conditions, and Evolution to inspect saved changes. Return after new eligible evidence arrives, select a current baseline, and generate a new reviewed comparison when useful.

Expected: Observations, interpretations, scenarios and your investment decision remain distinct. Optional monitoring has its own settings and may launch configured paid work; inspect those settings before enabling it.

If blocked / limits: The five-role investment committee, automatic accounting reconciliation, sector-specific models, licensed consensus feed and Monte Carlo are not delivered by this release. Do not treat this guide or an automation schedule as proof those workflows ran.

Free, isolated calculator check

Open the in-app workflow guide and choose Run isolated calculator check. This submits synthetic inputs to the nonpersistent preview endpoint; it saves no case, imports no source and launches no paid research.

Inputs: base revenue 1,000m, FY2025 → FY2027, CAGR 10%, EBITDA margin 20%, multiple 10×, net debt 200m, claims 50m, nonoperating assets 25m, shares 100m, reference price 20 USD. All three scenarios deliberately share inputs for this arithmetic check.
